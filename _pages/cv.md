Consultant cardiologist and physician-scientist, Heart Failure and Transplant Unit, Hospital Universitario Reina Sofía (Córdoba, Spain). A full publication list is on the [Publications](/publications/) page. ORCID: [0000-0001-8446-7369](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8446-7369).

Education
======
* MSc in Statistics for Clinical Trials, University College London (UCL), awarded 2025. Classification: Distinction. Research project at the MRC Clinical Trials Unit at UCL.
* PhD in Biomedicine, Universidad de Córdoba, 2022.
* Degree in Medicine, Universidad de Córdoba, 2010-2016. Premio Extraordinario de Fin de Carrera (top final-year award).
* Specialist training in Cardiology, Hospital Universitario Reina Sofía, Córdoba.

Research focus
======
Heart failure, cardio-oncology, ischaemic heart disease, randomised clinical trials, trial methodology and patient preferences. Author or co-author of more than 75 peer-reviewed publications, several as principal author and several in top-decile (D1) journals.
