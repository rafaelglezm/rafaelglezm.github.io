I am a consultant cardiologist and physician-scientist at the Heart Failure and Transplant Unit of **Hospital Universitario Reina Sofía** (Córdoba, Spain). My research sits at the interface between clinical cardiology and trial methodology, and is supported by a competitive **Juan Rodés contract** from the Instituto de Salud Carlos III (ISCIII), co-funded by the European Union.

I am an Emerging Investigator at the **Maimónides Biomedical Research Institute of Córdoba (IMIBIC)**, within the consolidated cardiovascular group GC-15, an affiliated investigator of the **CIBERCV** network, and a visiting investigator at the **Centro Nacional de Investigaciones Cardiovasculares (CNIC)** through the European COMPASS project (Innovative Health Initiative, Horizon Europe).

Research focus
======
My work is clinical rather than basic, and combines randomised clinical trials with real-world evidence, always with the aim of improving patient-centred outcomes. My main areas of interest are the most prevalent cardiovascular conditions, particularly **heart failure** and **ischaemic heart disease**, together with **cardio-oncology** and the study of vulnerable populations exposed to the cardiotoxic effects of cancer therapies. More recently I have developed a line of **clinical-trial methodology**, including the use of healthcare-systems data to design more efficient trials and the application of estimands such as the win ratio to composite endpoints.

Training
======
I hold a **PhD in Biomedicine** from the Universidad de Córdoba (2022) and an **MSc in Statistics for Clinical Trials** from **University College London (UCL)**, awarded with Distinction. My UCL research project, carried out during an ISCIII-funded mobility stay at the **MRC Clinical Trials Unit at UCL**, validated a computational algorithm for the identification of myocardial infarction in healthcare-systems data. I maintain an active methodological collaboration with the unit.

I am author or co-author of more than 75 peer-reviewed publications, several of them as principal (first or corresponding) author and a number in top-decile (D1) journals. A full list is available on the [Publications](/publications/) page.

Service and teaching
======
I serve as **Statistical Editor** of *JACC: Advances* and I am a board member of the **Young Cardiologists Group of the Spanish Society of Cardiology (SEC)**. Alongside my clinical and research activity, I supervise doctoral theses and undergraduate dissertations and teach within the Degree in Medicine at the Universidad de Córdoba. Details are on the [Teaching](/teaching/) and [CV](/cv/) pages.
