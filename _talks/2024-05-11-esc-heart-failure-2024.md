Presenter of several moderated posters at the 2024 Heart Failure Congress of the European Society of Cardiology:

- Differences in the clinical features of patients with heart failure and reduced, mildly reduced and preserved ejection fraction in a contemporary cohort in Spain.
- Differences in the optimisation of pharmacological treatment in patients with heart failure and reduced ejection fraction and advanced chronic kidney disease.
- Incidence of 1-year mortality, heart failure admissions and decompensation in patients followed in HF units in Spain, by type of unit.
- Optimisation of pharmacological treatment of heart failure in patients followed in specialised HF units in Spain.
- Prevalence and incidence of heart failure in patients with type 2 diabetes: results of the prospective DIABET-IC study.
